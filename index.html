-=- Today's Hottest Article -=-

A new Peroro & Friends arcade collaboration has officially opened in D.U., drawing large crowds of students on its first day of operation. The themed arcade, built around Kivotos’ popular mascot franchise, features a wide selection of rhythm games, claw machines, and co-op mini-games redesigned with Peroro and friends characters. The venue includes several limited-time attractions, such as exclusive prize capsules, interactive photo booths, and special “Friendship Combo” arcade cabinets that reward coordinated multiplayer play. Early visitors reported particularly long queues for the rare plush rewards and animated ticket redemption machines, which feature custom voice lines recorded for the collaboration.
