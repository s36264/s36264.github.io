News from the Districts

MOONLIGHT FESTIVAL

Shanhaijing's annual Moonlight Festival began this week, drawing thousands of visitors to its lantern-lined streets and bustling night markets. Traditional performances, tea ceremonies, and cultural exhibitions continued late into the evening as students celebrated one of the district's most anticipated events. The Black Tortoise Promenade described attendance as among the highest in recent years, with local businesses reporting record sales throughout the opening weekend. A minor sabotage attempt linked to Shintai Kai briefly disrupted early preparations, but it was quickly contained by Genryumon in cooperation with Schale. The festival resumed shortly after and continued smoothly into the night, with attendance remaining high and events proceeding as scheduled.

HIGHLANDER SPEED DEMONS

Highlander Academy's Transportation Bureau announced that an experimental high-speed train successfully exceeded the academy's previous rail speed record during a controlled test run. The achievement follows months of engineering work and infrastructure upgrades across Highlander's network. Officials stated that the data collected during the trial could contribute to future improvements in both passenger capacity and travel efficiency throughout Kivotos.

The test was conducted on a newly upgraded section of track equipped with advanced stabilization systems, allowing engineers to safely push the train beyond existing operational limits. Observers from several academies, including Hyakkiako and Trinity, were invited to witness the trial, which concluded without any major technical issues.

TSUKATSUKI RIO'S RETURN

Millennium students awoke to unexpected news this week as President Rio formally resumed her responsibilities within the Seminar. Her return follows a lengthy absence that fueled widespread speculation regarding the academy's leadership and future direction. While Rio has yet to make a comprehensive public statement, academy officials confirmed that she has already begun reviewing several major research, security, and infrastructure projects awaiting executive approval.
