Official Communication from the General Student Council

Kivotos Restoration Update

December 6th, 20XX

The General Student Council hereby announces that all restoration and stabilization efforts related to the Red Sky Incident have been formally completed as of this date. Over the course of the recovery period, coordinated reconstruction teams across all academies and districts have successfully restored critical infrastructure, including transportation networks, communication arrays, power distribution systems, and emergency response facilities. All temporary emergency substitutes implemented during the crisis phase have now been fully dismantled or integrated into permanent systems where appropriate. Comprehensive safety inspections conducted across multiple districts confirm that public facilities, academic institutions, and residential zones meet or exceed pre-incident operational standards. In addition, monitoring frameworks introduced during the restoration period will remain in place to ensure continued stability and rapid detection of any future anomalies. The General Student Council recognizes and extends its formal gratitude to all participating academies, student volunteer corps, engineering divisions, and logistics personnel whose sustained efforts contributed to the successful completion of this large-scale recovery operation. Their cooperation and discipline were essential in restoring normalcy across Kivotos. With this announcement, the General Student Council declares the restoration phase officially concluded and confirms the return of all districts to standard administrative and civilian operations.

It is additionally confirmed that all remaining districts affected by the Red Sky Incident have now been fully restored and returned to normal operational status, with no outstanding structural or systemic disruptions remaining.
