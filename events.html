Upcoming Events

29th Halo Festival

June 1st - June 14th

The 29th Halo Festival opens on June 1st, bringing students from across Kivotos together for two weeks of intense athletic competition. Expect everything from traditional track-and-field events to live-fire obstacle courses, tactical team contests, and academy rivalries on full display. Spectators are advised to arrive early due to increased security and crowd-control measures.

Several new events will debut during this year's festival, including an inter-academy drone racing league and a large-scale endurance challenge combining cycling, navigation, and survival tasks. Exhibition matches between academy champions are also planned, allowing spectators to watch some of Kivotos' most accomplished students compete outside the standard tournament brackets. This year also features expanded tactical events, including squad-based urban simulations and the Combined Operations Championship, where teams must complete chained objectives under strict time pressure.

Beyond the competitions, the festival grounds will host a wide range of attractions open to all attendees. These include academy exhibition pavilions, technology showcases, live demonstration arenas, and pop-up training courses where visitors can try modified versions of official events. Food districts, cultural performance stages, and night lantern displays will run throughout the festival, alongside merchandise stalls and inter-academy club exhibitions.

Grand Deathmatch

July 7th - July 24th

A competition devised by enterprising students of Gehenna Academy, the all-new Grand Deathmatch is starting on July 17th. Teams will compete in a controlled elimination tournament featuring urban, field & indoor combat simulations, resource scavenging, and increasingly chaotic arena conditions. This year's tournament will be fought across three distinct combat zones. The first stage will take place within the Ruins of Millennium, where damaged research facilities, collapsed structures, and abandoned urban sectors will provide opportunities for ambushes and close-quarters engagements. Competitors advancing from the opening rounds will then enter Gehenna's Volcanic Fields, a hazardous landscape of lava flows, ash storms, and unstable terrain designed to force constant movement and adaptation. The final stage will be held within Trinity's Catacombs, a sprawling underground maze of interconnected corridors, chambers, and dead ends. Organizers have stated that navigation, resource management, and reconnaissance will be just as important as direct combat in determining a victor.

Goliath Challenge

September 3rd - September 17th

The Abydos Desert Endurance Race begins on September 3rd, challenging riders to cross hundreds of kilometers of dunes, ruined highways, and sand-choked settlements. Competitors must rely on their own navigation skills and limited support stations while enduring Kivotos’ harshest desert conditions. Registration closes one week before the starting signal.

Organizers have extended the route for this year's race and introduced new navigation requirements intended to test riders' endurance, decision-making, and self-sufficiency. Security and rescue teams will be stationed along the course to assist competitors facing the desert's unpredictable conditions.

This year’s Abydos Desert Endurance Race begins at the Abydos–Gehenna border, sending riders deep into the desert on a demanding cross-district route. After the initial dune sections, competitors will pass through abandoned residential areas of Abydos, where collapsed buildings and buried streets create a difficult navigation zone. The course then continues alongside the Trans-Desert Railway, using long exposed rail stretches that test endurance under harsh wind and heat conditions. In the final stage, riders leave the tracks and descend into the Valley of the Student Council Presidents, where the finish line is set at the Hall.
